Business for Good & Project Design

July 9–12, 2026

Field Update

Y-CLAP《商业向善与公益项目设计》重庆模块圆满收官

July 2026 · Chongqing

From July 9 to 12, Y-CLAP participants and funder representatives gathered in Chongqing for a four-day, three-night immersion that moved from theory to action. The module combined cross-sector learning, field visits, project design practice, and closing pitches — turning climate awareness into concrete, youth-led solutions.

Key Highlights:

  • › Immersive opening at MiChuang, a public-interest co-working and learning space
  • › Sessions on business for good, systems thinking, and sustainable project design
  • › Field learning on the Yangtze River ecosystem and local conservation practice
  • › Final team pitches sharpened through mentor guidance and collaborative review

Source: CGPI/深圳国际公益学院 official WeChat feature, highlighting how young climate leaders move from learning to implementation.
